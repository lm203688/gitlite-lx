FlowTasks Refund Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-23

1. General Policy

FlowTasks is sold as a one-time purchase (Pro lifetime license). All Pro sales are final once the license is activated. We do not offer refunds for voluntary cancellations after activation.

2. EU Consumers — Right of Withdrawal Before Activation

Under EU Directive 2011/83/EU Article 11, EU consumers have a 14-day withdrawal right for digital content purchases. However, this right is lost once you begin downloading or activating the Pro license, provided you have given explicit consent and acknowledged that you thereby lose your withdrawal right.

If you wish to exercise your withdrawal right, you must do so before activating your Pro license. To withdraw, contact privacy@lxstudio.dev with your order reference within 14 calendar days of purchase.

3. Abandonment of the Right of Withdrawal for Digital Content

By clicking "Activate Pro License" in the FlowTasks extension, you confirm that:

  • You understand that activating your license causes you to lose your 14-day withdrawal right under EU Directive 2011/83/EU Article 11;
  • You expressly consent to immediate performance of the digital content (Pro features);
  • You acknowledge that your right of withdrawal is thereby waived.

4. Non-EU Consumers

If you reside outside the EU, your statutory refund rights (if any) are governed by your local jurisdiction. Please contact us before purchasing if you have concerns about refund eligibility.

5. How to Request a Refund

Email privacy@lxstudio.dev with:

  • Your order/reference number (from Paddle receipt)
  • Your reason for the refund request
  • Confirmation that you have not activated the Pro license (if claiming under EU law)

We aim to respond within 5 business days.
